ABOUT ME

From software to the energy field

More than seventeen years in tech: ten when I didn't yet know how to be kind to myself, and seven learning to walk back toward myself.

17+ years in tech Dowsing since 2023 Energy field harmonization

For a long time I lived on autopilot — in every area of my life. I delivered everything, met every deadline and everyone else’s expectations — and felt almost nothing. I believed that was part of adult life.

There came a moment when I realised how much I felt like a stranger in my own life. One day I woke up and hardly recognised anything as my own choice, and it was a great shock. Besides going through psychotherapy, I began to use integrative therapies to deal with the emotions that were out of balance, as part of the process. At some point I went from being a client to also being a practitioner, applying energy field harmonization to myself and to other people.

Energy field harmonization is what showed me the difference between getting through the days and being alive in them. Not because the noise stopped — it is still there. But because I can hear myself inside it again.

Today I guide sessions for people standing exactly where I stood: a full head, a full calendar and no room to hear themselves. The work is simple and asks nothing of you — only permission.

MY PATH

How I got here

  1. 2019

    A burnout crisis made me start paying more attention to myself. It was the beginning of an awakening.

  2. 2020

    I learned how much it matters to have professional support — and how to ask for help and to receive it.

  3. 2021

    I came into contact with the world of Integrative Therapies as a client of Acupuncture and Bach Flower Remedies. I was enchanted.

  4. 2022

    I began to venture in as a learner too, through several courses in the field, to look after my mental and emotional health during my career transition — still within tech.

  5. 2023

    I fell in love with Dowsing. Since then I have practised a lot, to look after myself and to help other people. In just a few years I accumulated more than 400 hours between training and practice — without noticing, and all within my regular working schedule.

  6. 2024

    I had my first contact with Reiki, to complement my dowsing. It was the starting point of an integration process that took hold later on and deepened my energy practice.

  7. 2025

    A year of processing and integrating all the changes up to that point. Of noticing how much more my life felt like mine, and how much I had learned to walk on my own two feet. That is why it was a year of deep reconnection with the will to live fully.

  8. 2026

    I started the year believing it would be my best year in tech, since my heart and mind were in the best shape of my life. But when I realised I was whole, I was no longer the person of 2022 who believed she wanted to build a new story in tech. Within a few months I realised that my cycle in tech had ended inside me. This kind of movement had already happened in other areas of my life since that awakening in 2019. There was no way not to do it. I had a wonderful farewell at the last tech company I worked for, and I began my path of opening my sessions to the world.

  9. Lately

    Life answered my movement and reconnected me with Reiki as my main tool to deliver this harmonization with even more depth — adding to the years of work with dowsing and energy. The results have surprised me.
