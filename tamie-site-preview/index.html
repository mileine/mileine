FREQUENTLY ASKED

What people usually ask me

Do I need to believe in something?
No. Harmonization asks for no faith, no religion, and no understanding of how it works. Only your permission.
Does it replace therapy or medical treatment?
It does not. This is a complementary practice — and it sits very comfortably alongside therapy, medication and medical care.
Do I need to be awake during the session?
No. You can be doing anything — working, commuting, resting. The work happens in the field, not in the physical body.
How many sessions do I need?
I usually suggest starting with one and seeing how it lands. If it makes sense to continue, we talk about a rhythm — normally once a month.
