WHEN TO HARMONIZE

When to harmonize?

You don't need to wait for a crisis to look after your energy field — but some signs usually show it is time to give it special attention:

  • A full mind, hard to slow down even when you “stop to rest”
  • The feeling of doing everything and not “getting anywhere”
  • Tiredness that doesn't go away even after resting
  • Difficulty making decisions, or overload, with your mind split between several fronts
  • Irritability or emotional sensitivity higher than usual
  • Feeling disconnected from yourself, “on autopilot”
  • Moments of transition (career change, grief, important decisions)

Harmonization doesn't change what is around you — the same demands, screens and people are still there. But it helps you reorganize how you inhabit that space: with more clarity, presence and lightness, even amid the rush. TECHNIQUES

Techniques used in the session

Dowsing

Dowsing is an integrative practice often described as a kind of “biosensor”.

Within this practice, our body and magnetic field are understood to constantly sense the vibrational frequencies around us — emotions, environments, thoughts and energies.

The perception picked up by the dowser is translated into micro-movements that make the pendulum move and help map the client's energy field.

Reiki

The word Reiki comes from Japanese — Rei means Universal Energy (the Force that guides Life) and Ki is our inner “vital energy”.

When routine gets intense or stress builds up, our vital energy tends to lose its harmony. We work with the intention that Reiki helps reorganize this flow and bring back a sense of lightness.

Within the Reiki tradition, energy is understood to transcend space and time — and that is what makes this work possible.

What I have heard from those who received it

Since 2023 I have received beautiful accounts from people who went through an Energy Alignment. I share some of the most recurring themes here, respecting the privacy of those who shared them:

  • A feeling of lightness, sometimes described as “never felt before”
  • A sense of sleeping better

  • More confidence and ease in expressing themselves
  • More energy and drive for everyday life
  • A feeling of well-being and happiness
  • More capacity to get through hard days without losing their way

  • Some describe effects that seem to last for weeks after the session, not only in the moment it happens

Beyond this feedback, some people who received a harmonization with me were so taken by the experience that they decided to learn dowsing too — and today they use the technique to look after themselves day to day. That makes me very happy, since the aim of this work is for more and more people to live more connected with themselves, and learning to look after your own energy is part of that path.

These are individual accounts. They are not promises or guarantees of results — experiences differ from person to person.
