Distance Reiki: how the energy reaches you

When routine gets intense or stress builds up, our vital energy tends to lose its harmony. Reiki works to reorganize that flow.

By Tamie September 20, 2026 · 5 min read

What does “Reiki” mean?

The word Reiki comes from Japanese. Rei means Universal Energy (the Force that guides Life) and Ki is our inner “vital energy”.

How does it work at a distance?

We often imagine care as something in person, but energy is not limited by physical distance. Think of distance Reiki as a radio frequency or a Wi-Fi signal: you cannot see the radio wave, but the music plays clearly on your speaker when it is tuned to the right frequency. In the same way, Reiki energy is directed in a focused, intentional way straight to your energy field.

Reiki energy transcends space and time.

That is the understanding behind Reiki, and it is what makes this work possible.

Asynchronous distance Reiki

In the harmonization sessions I use asynchronous distance Reiki: a practical and gentle way to receive energetic care at your own pace, without having to adapt your calendar or pause your commitments. A time is reserved just for me to channel and send the energy to your field, while you carry on with your normal routine — working, resting, looking after the house or even sleeping.

  • No need to pause. You do not need to stop what you are doing, meditate or be in a quiet place when the energy is sent. It is directed with clear intention and acts in a fluid, subtle way in your field.
  • Natural absorption. Your energy system receives the frequency at the pace and time in which it is most receptive, acting exactly where there is a need for balance, relief of tension or vibrational reorganization.

It was designed to respect your time and your daily routine: care that adapts to you, not the other way round.

Where Reiki fits in a session

An Energy Alignment has three stages. First comes energy mapping through dowsing. Then comes the Reiki harmonization: the themes mapped are cared for with distance Reiki, to tune your field to the frequency of Universal Energy. The third stage is synthesis and clarity: I put together the main information from the session, comparing your field before and after, to help you anchor that presence in your day-to-day.

After the session

Receiving a session is only one way of looking after your energy field. It is natural for the reading to move between before and after the Reiki, but keeping the result depends on daily care. The best way to look after your field is to get to know yourself so you can feel and give it what it needs at each moment: rest, water, fun, time with people you love, knowledge, silence.

How I got here

More recently, life answered my movement and reconnected me with Reiki as my main tool to deliver this harmonization with even more depth, adding to my years of work with dowsing and energy. I did the Reiki Level I, II and Master/Teacher certification in 2026. The whole path is on the About page.
