Dowsing: the pendulum as a map of your field

Our body and our magnetic field are constantly picking up what is around us. Dowsing is a way of giving those perceptions a voice.

By Tamie September 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Our body and our magnetic field constantly perceive the vibrational frequencies around us — emotions, environments, thoughts and energies. But with a fast routine we do not always manage to translate these sensations into the conscious mind. Dowsing is one way of doing that translation.

What is dowsing?

Dowsing is an integrative practice that works as a kind of “biosensor”. The word comes from two roots: radius (ray, radiation) and aisthesis (sensitivity). Put together, it means “sensitivity to radiations or energies”.

And the pendulum?

The pendulum is one of the main tools of dowsing. It is important to remember that tools like the pendulum have no magical power of their own. They work as an extension of the dowser: a visual pointer in front of questions or research charts. For example: “does this chakra show any imbalance?” or “which themes need care right now?”.

The dowser's involuntary micro-movements of the muscles respond to oscillations in the client's energy. That is what makes the pendulum move, and it allows the dowser to map blockages, excesses or stagnation in the energy field.

It is not for making predictions or rigid labels, but for bringing light to what your field is asking for in the moment.

How I use it in a harmonization

In an Energy Alignment session, dowsing is the first stage: a navigation map. I use it to read and analyse the current state of your field, looking at two things:

  • Chakras: the energy centres, and whether each one is balanced, blocked or overactive.
  • Yin and Yang: the balance between action, movement and expansion (Yang) and rest, receptivity and inner withdrawal (Yin).

Our work is not to change your emotions or your energy, but to recognise where you are and what you need right now — on the physical, mental, emotional and spiritual levels. The themes mapped are then cared for with Reiki, and you receive a report comparing your field before and after the session. You can read how that second stage works in the article on distance Reiki.

What dowsing is not

  • It does not predict the future.
  • It does not put people into fixed labels.
  • It does not make clinical diagnoses, and it does not replace medical, psychiatric or psychological care.
